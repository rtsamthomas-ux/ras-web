A modern, responsive website for the IEEE Robotics and Automation Society, built with Next.js, TypeScript and Three.js from publicly available information, and deployed on Vercel as a static site.
- Hero: an interactive 3D 4-DOF robot arm driven by analytic inverse kinematics. It follows the cursor, and you click to pick up and place blocks that fall, stack and tip with simple physics, with live joint-angle telemetry. It runs an automatic demo on mobile.
- Content: RAS mission, vision and field of interest; a history timeline from the 1984 council to ICRA 2027; current leadership; all ten journals (each linking to its official page and IEEE Xplore) with an RA-L spotlight and IEEE robotics standards; ICRA/IROS/CASE with a live ICRA 2027 countdown plus 10 more sponsored conferences; 47 technical committees, 220+ chapters, grants and education programmes; the 12 Society awards; membership; and an FAQ. Every card opens its official page when clicked (90+ verified links), and publications have Open buttons. All facts are kept in one content file with their public sources, which are listed in the footer.
- Design: an original engineering-blueprint look (grid paper, serif + grotesk type, signal-orange accents) with full light/dark themes, scroll-reveal animations and count-up stats.
- Quality: responsive from 360 px phones to wide screens, accessible (semantic HTML, skip link, keyboard-friendly, reduced-motion support) and fast (static export, self-hosted fonts, 3D lazy-loaded and paused when off-screen).
